# TRANSLATION STANDARD
Version: 1.0
Project: Sindh Local Government Act 2013

## Purpose

This standard defines the official translation methodology for all legal documents in this repository.

## Supported Languages

- English
- Urdu
- Sindhi

## Translation Principles

- Preserve the original legal meaning.
- Do not simplify statutory language.
- Do not add personal interpretation.
- Do not omit legal words.
- Keep terminology consistent throughout the repository.

## Translation Order

English (Official Text)

↓

Urdu Translation

↓

Sindhi Translation

## Legal Terminology

Use official legal terminology wherever available.

If no official translation exists:

- Keep the original English legal term.
- Add a short explanation if necessary.

## Verification

Every translation must be marked as one of the following:

Verified

Translation Review Pending

Official Verification Required

## Formatting

Each Section should contain:

Section Number

English

Urdu

Sindhi

Notes (if required)

## Prohibited

Never translate by guessing.

Never change legal intent.

Never rewrite statutory language.

Never omit definitions.

## Quality Standard

Accuracy

Consistency

Neutrality

Legal Precision

Official terminology first.
