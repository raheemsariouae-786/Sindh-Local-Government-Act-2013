# AMENDMENT STANDARD

## Pakistan Legal AI Framework (PLAIS)

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This standard establishes the framework for researching, documenting and analysing amendments to Pakistani laws, with particular emphasis on the Sindh Local Government Act, 2013.

---

# Primary Sources

Always use:

1. Official Act
2. Official Amendment Act
3. Official Gazette
4. Official Notifications
5. Constitution of Pakistan
6. Supreme Court Judgments
7. Sindh High Court Judgments

---

# Amendment Research Standard

Every amendment analysis must identify:

- Name of the Act
- Amendment Act
- Amendment Year
- Relevant Section
- Nature of Amendment
- Effective Date
- Legal Effect

---

# Amendment Documentation

Every amendment should clearly show:

Original Text

↓

Amended Text

↓

Reason for Amendment

↓

Practical Effect

---

# Verification Standard

Never invent:

- Amendment Acts
- Amendment Dates
- Sections
- Gazette References
- Court Decisions

If official verification is unavailable, state:

Official verification is required.

---

# Output Standard

Every amendment summary should include:

1. Original Provision
2. Amended Provision
3. Legal Reason
4. Effective Date
5. Practical Effect
6. Verification Status

---

# Golden Rule

Always compare the original law with the latest officially amended version.

Verification before publication.
