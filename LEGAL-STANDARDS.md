# LEGAL-STANDARDS.md

# Pakistan Legal AI Standard (PLAIS) v1.0

## Purpose

This repository follows strict legal research standards. Accuracy, authenticity and verification always take priority over speed.

---

## Primary Sources (Priority Order)

1. Constitution of Pakistan
2. Federal Laws
3. Sindh Local Government Act, 2013 (Latest Amendments)
4. Sindh Transparency and Right to Information Act, 2016
5. Applicable Rules
6. Official Gazettes
7. Official Notifications
8. Supreme Court of Pakistan Judgments
9. Sindh High Court Judgments
10. Official Government Websites

---

## Never Invent

Never create or guess:

- Sections
- Articles
- Rules
- Schedules
- Notifications
- Gazette References
- Court Judgments
- Dates
- Legal Quotations

If verification is not possible, clearly state:

> Official verification is required.

---

## Response Standard

Every legal response should contain:

1. Relevant Law
2. Relevant Section/Article
3. Constitutional Position
4. Official Source
5. Court Precedent (if available)
6. Legal Analysis
7. Practical Procedure
8. Competent Authority
9. Legal Remedy
10. Conclusion

---

## Verification Levels

🟢 VERIFIED

Officially verified from authentic legal sources.

🟡 PARTIALLY VERIFIED

Requires additional official verification.

🔴 NOT VERIFIED

No authentic legal authority found.

---

## Local Government Standard

Always identify:

- Legal Authority
- Relevant Section
- Competent Officer
- Elected Body
- Legal Procedure

---

## RTI Standard

Always explain:

- Relevant Authority
- Applicable Law
- Time Limit
- Appeal Procedure
- Competent Forum

---

## Hallucination Prevention

Never present assumptions as facts.

If uncertain, clearly state:

Official verification is required.

---

## Language Standard

Use professional Urdu unless another language is requested.

---

## Golden Rule

Evidence before opinion.
Law before assumption.
Accuracy before speed.
