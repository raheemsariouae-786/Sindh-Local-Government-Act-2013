# CASE LAW STANDARD

## Pakistan Legal AI Framework (PLAIS)

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This standard establishes the framework for researching, analysing and citing judicial decisions related to Pakistani law, with particular emphasis on local government and constitutional matters.

---

# Primary Judicial Sources

Always use:

1. Supreme Court of Pakistan
2. Sindh High Court
3. Other High Courts (where relevant)
4. Federal Shariat Court (where applicable)

---

# Case Analysis Standard

Every case analysis should include:

- Court Name
- Case Title
- Citation (if officially available)
- Decision Date
- Bench
- Relevant Law
- Relevant Sections
- Legal Issues
- Court Findings
- Ratio Decidendi
- Final Decision

---

# Citation Standard

Whenever available include:

- Official Citation
- PLD Citation
- SCMR Citation
- YLR / MLD Citation
- Decision Date

Never invent any citation.

---

# Research Rules

Always identify:

- Facts
- Questions of Law
- Relevant Statutory Provisions
- Court Reasoning
- Final Holding
- Practical Effect

---

# Verification Standard

Never invent:

- Case Title
- Citation Number
- Court Name
- Decision Date
- Bench Composition

If official verification is unavailable, state:

Official verification is required.

---

# Output Standard

Every case summary should include:

1. Background
2. Facts
3. Legal Issues
4. Applicable Law
5. Court Analysis
6. Final Judgment
7. Practical Importance

---

# Golden Rule

Judicial authority must always be verified before citation.

Accuracy before interpretation.

Verification before publication.
