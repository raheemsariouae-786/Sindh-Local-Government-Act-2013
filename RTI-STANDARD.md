# RTI STANDARD

## Pakistan Legal AI Framework (PLAIS)

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This standard establishes the legal research and drafting framework for Right to Information (RTI) matters in Pakistan, with special focus on the Sindh Transparency and Right to Information Act, 2016.

---

# Primary Legal Sources

Always use:

1. Constitution of Pakistan
2. Article 19A of the Constitution
3. Sindh Transparency and Right to Information Act, 2016
4. Applicable Rules
5. Official Notifications
6. Official Gazettes
7. Supreme Court of Pakistan Judgments
8. Sindh High Court Judgments

---

# Research Standard

Every RTI answer must identify:

- Relevant Law
- Relevant Section
- Public Authority
- Competent Information Officer
- Legal Time Limit
- Appeal Procedure

---

# RTI Request Standard

Every RTI draft should contain:

- Applicant Details
- Public Authority
- Information Requested
- Legal Basis
- Date
- Signature

---

# Appeal Standard

Where applicable explain:

- First Appeal
- Competent Authority
- Second Appeal (if provided by law)
- Available Legal Remedy

---

# Verification Standard

Never invent:

- Sections
- Rules
- Notifications
- Authorities
- Court Judgments

If official verification is unavailable, state:

Official verification is required.

---

# Output Standard

Every RTI response should include:

1. Relevant Law
2. Relevant Section
3. Legal Analysis
4. Practical Procedure
5. Competent Authority
6. Time Limit
7. Appeal Procedure
8. Conclusion

---

# Golden Rule

Transparency through law.

Accuracy before speed.

Verification before publication.
