# CITATION STANDARD

## Pakistan Legal AI Framework (PLAIS)

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This standard defines how all legal authorities shall be cited throughout this repository.

The objective is to ensure consistency, transparency, legal authenticity and verifiability.

---

# Citation Priority

Always cite legal authorities in the following order:

1. Constitution of Pakistan
2. Federal Statutes
3. Provincial Statutes
4. Rules
5. Regulations
6. Official Gazettes
7. Official Notifications
8. Supreme Court of Pakistan Judgments
9. High Court Judgments
10. Official Government Publications

---

# Constitution Citation

Format:

Constitution of Pakistan, Article __

Example:

Constitution of Pakistan, Article 140A

---

# Statute Citation

Format:

Law Name, Section __

Example:

Sindh Local Government Act, 2013, Section 74

---

# Rules Citation

Format:

Rules Name, Rule __

Example:

Sindh Local Councils Rules, Rule 25

---

# Gazette Citation

Whenever available include:

• Gazette Number

• Gazette Date

• Publishing Authority

---

# Notification Citation

Always mention:

• Notification Number

• Date

• Issuing Department

• Legal Authority

---

# Court Judgment Citation

Whenever available include:

• Court Name

• Case Title

• Citation Number

• Decision Date

• Legal Principle

Never invent any case citation.

---

# Amendment Citation

Always specify:

Original Provision

Amended Provision

Effective Date

Legal Effect

---

# Verification Standard

Every citation must be capable of official verification.

If official verification is unavailable, state:

Official verification is required.

---

# Citation Principles

Accuracy

Authenticity

Neutrality

Transparency

Consistency

---

# Never Cite

Never cite:

Unverified blogs

Social media posts

Unofficial summaries

Anonymous sources

AI-generated references without verification

---

# Repository Standard

All legal documents within this repository shall follow this citation standard.
