## 🇬🇧 English

### SCHEDULE-VIII: Matters Respecting Which Bye-laws May Be Made
> **Source:** Fully verified, word-for-word — Judges' Library, High Court of Sindh, Karachi.

1. Registration of births, deaths and marriages.
2. Regulation of cattle/animal sale.
3. Regulation/management of orphanages, widow homes, and other relief institutions.
4. Village defence organization; village safety/security measures.
5. Regulation/management of common property.
6. Regulation of burial/burning grounds.
7. Regulation of animal slaughter; construction/maintenance of slaughter houses.
8. Detention/destruction of stray dogs.
9. Enforcement of vaccination.
10. Prevention/control of infectious diseases.
11. Prevention of food adulteration.
12. Regulation of milk supply.
13. Regulation of stables.
14. Prevention of encroachments.
15. Prevention/abatement of nuisances.
16. Regulation of building erection/re-erection.
17. Regulation of dangerous buildings/structures.
18. Management/regulation of public ferries.
19. Regulation of dangerous/offensive trades.
20. Management/regulation of cattle pounds.
21. Traffic regulation.
22. Organization/regulation of fairs, shows, tournaments and public gatherings.
23. Enforcement of compulsory education.
24. Specifying purposes requiring licences, and licence terms/conditions.
25. Promoting/furthering Council functions and Act purposes not covered by rules.

**Source:** Judges' Library, High Court of Sindh, Karachi (official compiled PDF, provided by the user) — Sindh Act No. XLII of 2013

10. وچڙندڙ بيمارين جي روڪٿام.
11. خوراڪ ۾ مليشڪاري جي روڪٿام.
12. کير جي فراهمي جو ضابطو.
13. طبيلي جو ضابطو.
14. تجاوزات جي روڪٿام.
15. تڪليف جي روڪٿام.
16. عمارتن جي تعمير جو ضابطو.
17. خطرناڪ عمارتن جو ضابطو.
18. عوامي فيرين جو انتظام.
19. خطرناڪ ڌنڌن جو ضابطو.
20. ڪيٽل پائونڊ جو انتظام.
21. ٽرئفڪ جو ضابطو.
22. ميلن جي تنظيم.
23. لازمي تعليم جو نفاذ.

25. ڪائونسل جي فرضن جو فروغ.
