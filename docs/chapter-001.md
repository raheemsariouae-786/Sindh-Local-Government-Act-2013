## 🇬🇧 English — CHAPTER-I: INTRODUCTION
### (Sections 1–7)

> **Verification status:** This chapter has been checked word-for-word against the user-supplied official PDF (Judges' Library, High Court of Sindh, Karachi, compiled May 2016). It reflects amendments up to Act No. XXXVIII/2015. **This is a correction of an earlier version, which gave only a partial/selected definitions list — the full list of ~100 definitions is included below.**

**1. Short title, extent and commencement.**
(1) This Act may be called the Sindh Local Government Act, 2013.
(2) It shall extend to the whole of the Province of Sindh.
(3) It shall come into force at once.

**2. Exception.** Government may, if deemed necessary in the public interest, after recording reasons, exclude any area(s) from the operation of any provision(s) of this Act, and may make alternate provisions for such area by rules as deemed appropriate.

- (lxxxix) **"Union Council"** — constituted under this Act.
- (xc) **"urban area"** — recognized/declared as such under this Act.
- (xci) **"Urban Council"** — a town committee, municipal committee, municipal corporation, or metropolitan corporation.
- (xcii) **"Vehicle"** — a wheeled conveyance capable of use on a street.
- (xciii) *[Omitted]*
- (xciv) **"village"** — a revenue estate area under the Sindh Land Revenue Act, 1967.
- (xcv) **"voter"** — a person whose name appears on the electoral rolls prepared/adopted under the Act.
- (xcvi) **"water works"** — includes a lake, stream, spring, well, pump, reservoir, cistern, tank, duct, sluice, pipe, engine, or other water-supply appliance.
- (xcvii) **"way"** — a road, footpath, square, alley or passage leading to a public way or not.
- (xcviii) *[Omitted]*
- (xcix) **"ward"** — a basic electoral unit under this Act.
- (c) **"youth"** *(added by Act No. XXXVIII/2015, dated 27/8/2015)* — a person below 25 years of age at the time of filing nomination papers.

**4. Succession.** The Councils established under this Act succeed to the rights, assets and liabilities of the councils established under the earlier Local Government Laws in the Province.

**5. Council to work within the framework of the Provincial Government.**
(1) Councils shall function within the provincial framework and faithfully observe Federal and Provincial Laws.
(2) In performing their functions, Councils shall not impede or prejudice Government's exercise of executive authority.

**6.** *[Omitted by Act No. LIII/2013, dated 2/11/2013]*

**7. Councils to be a body corporate.**
(1) A Council is a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal, with power (subject to the Act and rules) to acquire, hold, and alienate property (movable and immovable), and to sue and be sued in its own name.
(2) The seal follows a Council-approved description and remains in the Chief Executive's custody, who is responsible for its proper use.
