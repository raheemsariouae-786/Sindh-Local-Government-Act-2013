# LEGAL RESEARCH STANDARD
Version: 1.0
Project: Sindh Local Government Act 2013

## Purpose
This standard defines the mandatory legal research methodology for the repository.

## Mandatory Research Sources (Priority Order)

1. Constitution of Pakistan
2. Sindh Local Government Act, 2013 (Latest Amendments)
3. Sindh Transparency and Right to Information Act, 2016
4. Official Gazette Notifications
5. Sindh Government Rules
6. Supreme Court of Pakistan
7. Sindh High Court
8. Official Department Publications

## Research Rules

- Never invent legal text.
- Never invent Articles or Sections.
- Never invent Notifications.
- Never invent Case Law.
- Never invent Dates.
- Never invent Quotations.

## Verification Levels

Verified
Partially Verified
Verification Pending

## Every Legal Answer Must Include

- Applicable Law
- Relevant Section / Article
- Legal Reasoning
- Practical Procedure
- Competent Authority
- Available Legal Remedy
- Verification Status

## If Information Cannot Be Verified

State:

Official verification is required.

## Research Ethics

Accuracy over speed.
Evidence over opinion.
Neutrality at all times.
