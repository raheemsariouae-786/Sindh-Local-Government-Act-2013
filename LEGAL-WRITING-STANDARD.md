# LEGAL WRITING STANDARD
Version: 1.0
Project: Sindh Local Government Act 2013

## Purpose

This document establishes the official legal writing standard for the repository.

---

## Writing Principles

- Write with legal accuracy.
- Use clear and professional language.
- Preserve the exact legal meaning.
- Separate verified facts from interpretation.
- Maintain neutrality at all times.

---

## Mandatory Structure

Every legal document should contain:

1. Title
2. Applicable Law
3. Relevant Section(s)
4. Constitutional Reference (if applicable)
5. Legal Analysis
6. Practical Procedure
7. Competent Authority
8. Legal Remedy
9. Verification Status
10. References

---

## Legal Analysis Rules

Always distinguish between:

- Verified Legal Text
- Legal Interpretation
- Opinion

These three categories must never be mixed.

---

## Citation Rules

Every legal statement should be supported by:

- Constitution of Pakistan
- Sindh Local Government Act, 2013
- Sindh Transparency & Right to Information Act, 2016
- Official Gazette
- Official Rules
- Verified Court Judgment (if applicable)

---

## Prohibited

Never invent:

- Sections
- Articles
- Notifications
- Gazette References
- Court Judgments
- Dates
- Legal Quotations

If verification is unavailable, clearly state:

**Official verification is required.**

---

## Writing Style

Use:

- Short paragraphs
- Clear headings
- Professional legal terminology
- Consistent formatting

Avoid:

- Emotional language
- Political bias
- Personal opinion
- Unsupported claims

---

## Verification Status

Every document must include one of the following:

- Verified
- Partially Verified
- Verification Pending
- Official Verification Required

---

## Quality Standard

- Accuracy
- Consistency
- Neutrality
- Transparency
- Legal Precision
- Evidence-Based Research
