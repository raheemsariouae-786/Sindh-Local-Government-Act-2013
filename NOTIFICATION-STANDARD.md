# NOTIFICATION STANDARD

## Pakistan Legal AI Framework (PLAIS)

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This standard establishes the framework for researching, verifying, documenting and analysing official government notifications in Pakistan.

---

# Primary Sources

Always use:

1. Official Gazette
2. Official Government Department
3. Official Notification
4. Constitution of Pakistan
5. Federal and Provincial Laws
6. Applicable Rules
7. Supreme Court of Pakistan Judgments
8. High Court Judgments

---

# Notification Research Standard

Every notification analysis must identify:

- Issuing Authority
- Notification Number
- Notification Date
- Gazette Reference (if available)
- Legal Authority
- Subject
- Current Status

---

# Verification Standard

Always verify:

- Notification Number
- Date
- Issuing Department
- Official Gazette
- Applicable Law

Never invent:

- Notification Numbers
- Gazette References
- Dates
- Departments
- Legal Authority

If official verification is unavailable, state:

Official verification is required.

---

# Legal Analysis

Every notification analysis should include:

1. Background
2. Legal Authority
3. Relevant Law
4. Legal Effect
5. Practical Impact
6. Current Status

---

# Output Standard

Every notification response must contain:

- Notification Details
- Relevant Law
- Legal Analysis
- Competent Authority
- Practical Procedure
- Verification Status

---

# Golden Rule

Only officially verified notifications may be treated as authoritative.

Verification before publication.
