# CONSTITUTION STANDARD

## Pakistan Legal AI Framework (PLAIS)

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This standard establishes the framework for researching, analysing and citing the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.

---

# Primary Sources

Always use:

1. Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan (latest official text)
2. Constitutional Amendments
3. Supreme Court of Pakistan Judgments
4. High Court Judgments
5. Official Gazettes
6. Official Notifications

---

# Constitutional Research Standard

Every constitutional answer must identify:

- Relevant Article
- Constitutional Principle
- Applicable Amendment
- Relevant Judicial Interpretation
- Competent Authority
- Available Constitutional Remedy

---

# Constitutional Citation Standard

Always cite:

Constitution of Pakistan, Article __

Example:

Constitution of Pakistan, Article 19A

Constitution of Pakistan, Article 140A

Never invent constitutional provisions.

---

# Constitutional Analysis

Every analysis should include:

1. Constitutional Background
2. Relevant Article(s)
3. Legal Interpretation
4. Judicial Interpretation
5. Practical Application
6. Conclusion

---

# Constitutional Remedies

Where applicable explain:

- Constitutional Petition
- Writ Jurisdiction
- Judicial Review
- Fundamental Rights
- Competent Court

---

# Verification Standard

Never invent:

- Articles
- Amendments
- Constitutional Judgments
- Official Notifications

If official verification is unavailable, state:

Official verification is required.

---

# Output Standard

Every constitutional response should contain:

- Relevant Article
- Constitutional Principle
- Legal Analysis
- Judicial Position
- Practical Procedure
- Available Remedy

---

# Golden Rule

The Constitution is the supreme law of Pakistan.

Constitution before statute.

Verification before publication.
